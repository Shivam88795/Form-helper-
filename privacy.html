Privacy Policy

Draft template — publish karne se pehle apne actual business details ke saath review karein.

Data we collect

If enabled, account authentication uses your email address. Membership requests may store account ID, selected plan, amount, UPI transaction reference, status and timestamp. The eligibility checker currently runs in your browser and does not intentionally send your entered details to a server.

Payment safety

Never ask for or collect UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, banking password, or full payment credentials. UPI references should be accessed only by authorized staff and retained only as long as necessary.

Purpose and sharing

Data is used to manage accounts, verify membership payments, provide requested assistance, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations. Do not sell personal data. Limit access to authorized administrators.

Retention and requests

Set a retention period, contact email and process for access/deletion requests before launch. Firebase and hosting providers may process data according to their own terms.

Contact

Replace help@example.com with your real business contact email and publish the date this policy takes effect.
